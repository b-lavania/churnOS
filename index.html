Churn Analysis

A comprehensive diagnostic suite for understanding customer attrition before it impacts your bottom line.

Simulation Inputs
  • Base churn multiplier & seasonal effects
  • Premium segment mix (%)
  • 'Subscribe & Save' adoption rates
Generated Outputs
  • Kaplan-Meier survival curves by cohort
  • Revenue vs. Logo churn comparison
  • Random Forest feature importance (Churn Drivers)
The Operator's Edge Stop guessing why users leave. By simulating the exact impact of retention programs (like 'Subscribe & Save'), you can definitively prove whether sacrificing initial margin is worth the flattened survival curve.
🔁

Retention & Unit Economics

The ultimate LTV:CAC dashboard linking acquisition costs directly to long-term profitability.

Simulation Inputs
  • Average Order Value (AOV) & Discount Freq
  • Refund Rates (%) & COGS (%)
  • Blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Generated Outputs
  • Triangular cohort heatmaps (Day-N Retention)
  • True Margin Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
  • Live, dynamic LTV:CAC Ratio tracking
The Operator's Edge Revenue isn't margin. This module hard-links your marketing spend (CAC) and fulfillment costs (COGS/Refunds) to your retention data, revealing your *actual* payback period and preventing unprofitable scaling.
⚡

Conversion Optimization

Identify and plug the exact leaks in your user journey from first visit to final purchase.

Simulation Inputs
  • Total session volume (N)
  • Step-by-step dropoff parameters
  • 'Free Shipping' threshold toggle
Generated Outputs
  • Visit-to-Purchase funnel visualizations
  • Segmented conversion rates (Mobile vs Desktop)
  • Statistical significance (A/B Test Z-scores)
The Operator's Edge Is offering free shipping worth the margin hit? Simulate the exact volume increase needed to offset the shipping cost, and instantly validate if your new checkout flow is statistically significantly better.
💰

Marketplace Pricing Analytics

Optimize your platform take-rate without destroying seller liquidity or buyer demand.

Simulation Inputs
  • Seller volume & Tier distributions
  • Take Rate multiplier & Fixed Transaction Fees
  • Buyer vs. Seller fee splits
Generated Outputs
  • Price Elasticity of Demand curves
  • Effective Take Rate & Gross Merchandise Volume
  • Revenue breakdown by commission tier
The Operator's Edge Marketplaces are delicate ecosystems. Use this module to safely simulate raising transaction fees or shifting the burden from seller to buyer, ensuring you maximize revenue capture without triggering a mass seller exodus.
