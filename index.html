Causal Business Modeling

A centralized engine linking CAC, churn, and AOV directly to your lifetime profitability and bottom line.

Simulation Inputs
  • Blended CAC & Base Churn
  • Average Order Value (AOV)
  • Gross Margin %
Generated Outputs
  • LTV to CAC Sensitivity Matrix
  • Executive Summary Dashboard
  • Global Causal Graph
Directed acyclic graph (DAG) architecture links every input to outputs via deterministic causal chains, not correlations. Changing one parameter propagates through the entire model in real time.
🔮

Bayesian Attribution (MMM)

PyMC-based Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) for true causal insights into your advertising spend and ROI.

Simulation Inputs
  • Ad Spend per Channel
  • Adstock Decay Parameters
  • Saturation Coefficients
Generated Outputs
  • Diminishing Returns Curves
  • Causal Spend Effectiveness
  • Budget Optimization Scenarios
PyMC probabilistic model with adstock decay (geometric/Weibull) and Hill saturation curves. Posterior sampling via MCMC quantifies budget uncertainty, far beyond deterministic attribution.

E-Commerce & RFM Analysis

Advanced segmentation and inventory volatility modeling explicitly designed for modern D2C storefronts.

Simulation Inputs
  • Refund Rate Volatility
  • Unit COGS & Shipping
  • Time-since-last-purchase
Generated Outputs
  • RFM Customer Segments
  • Inventory Impact on Margin
  • Repurchase Propensity
RFM scoring with recency-weighted segmentation. Inventory volatility is modeled as a margin shock propagated causally through the DAG: directly surfacing the P&L impact of supply disruptions.

Marketplace Liquidity

Model the delicate balance of supply vs demand health and stimulate network effect flywheels.

Simulation Inputs
  • Buyer-to-Seller Ratios
  • Cross-Side Network Params
  • Platform Subsidy %
Generated Outputs
  • Liquidity Health Metrics

Churn Analysis

A comprehensive diagnostic suite for understanding customer attrition before it impacts your bottom line.

Simulation Inputs
  • Base churn multiplier
  • Premium segment mix (%)
  • 'Subscribe & Save' adoption
Generated Outputs
  • Kaplan-Meier survival curves
  • Revenue vs Logo churn
  • Random Forest Churn Drivers
Kaplan-Meier non-parametric survival estimator with log-rank hypothesis tests. Random Forest feature importances identify top churn drivers, without assuming proportional hazards.

Retention & Unit Economics

The ultimate LTV:CAC dashboard linking acquisition costs directly to long-term profitability.

Simulation Inputs
  • Average Order Value (AOV)
  • Refund Rates & COGS
  • Blended CAC
Generated Outputs
  • Cohort heatmaps (Day-N)

Cohort heatmaps built from first-purchase date bucketing. CLV uses margin-adjusted discounted cash flow across the simulated survival curve, not naive revenue multiples.

Conversion Optimization

Identify and plug the exact leaks in your user journey from first visit to final purchase.

Simulation Inputs
  • Total session volume (N)
  • Step-by-step dropoff
  • 'Free Shipping' threshold
Generated Outputs
  • Visit-to-Purchase funnels
  • Segmented conversion rates
  • A/B Test Z-scores
Two-proportion z-test for A/B significance with Bonferroni correction for MVT. Sample size calculator uses full power analysis: explicitly surfacing Type I / Type II error tradeoffs.

Marketplace Pricing

Optimize your platform take-rate without destroying seller liquidity or buyer demand.

Simulation Inputs
  • Seller volume & Tiers
  • Take Rate multiplier
  • Buyer vs. Seller fee splits
Generated Outputs
  • Price Elasticity of Demand
  • Effective Take Rate & GMV
  • Revenue by commission tier
Price elasticity modeled via log-linear demand curves. Commission tier optimization uses constrained revenue maximization to find the take-rate ceiling, without triggering seller-exit risk.
